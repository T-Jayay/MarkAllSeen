# Mark All Seen

Adds a **Mark all as seen** button to the mod settings that clears the "New!" markers on your profile in one click: logbook entries (items and equipment, monsters, environments, survivors, drones and challenges) and the survivors, skills and skins in character select. Requires [Risk of Options](https://thunderstore.io/c/riskofrain2/p/Rune580/Risk_Of_Options/).

## How to use
Settings → Mod Options → **MarkAllSeen** → **Mark all**, on the title screen or during a run. A popup says how many entries were marked, and your profile is saved straight away.

## Details
- Only what is showing "New!" right now is marked. Anything you unlock or discover later still gets its "New!" marker as normal.
- Survivors you haven't unlocked, or whose DLC you don't own, keep their "New!" markers, skills and skins included, for when you get them.
- There is no undo. The game's console command `viewables_clear_viewed` does the opposite: it brings back the "New!" marker on everything, including what you had already looked at.
- Entries whose internal names the game can't save (such as names with spaces) keep their "New!" marker.

## Multiplayer
Client-side: it only changes your own profile, so nobody else in your lobby needs it.
